# OmniMem

🧠 **OmniMem** is an intelligent tool that combines Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) and AI to capture, understand, and archive your thoughts.

### 🛠️ How It Works

* **Recording:** Click the microphone icon or use a keyboard shortcut.
* **Transcription (ASR):** Your spoken words are instantly transformed into text.
* **Display (Visual):** The text scrolls across your screen with key terms highlighted.
* **Memorization:** The AI summarizes the core idea and saves it to your personal knowledge base.
